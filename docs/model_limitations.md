# GlycoTwin Model Limitations & Safety Notice

## 1. Research Disclaimer & Regulatory Status

- **Non-Clinical Research Prototype**: GlycoTwin is strictly an academic research demonstration developed using retrospective observational data.
- **No Medical Advice**: Predictions generated by this system do not constitute medical advice, clinical diagnoses, or therapeutic recommendations.
- **No Hypoglycemia Prevention Claim**: The system does not prevent hypoglycemia; it evaluates statistical correlations between historical telemetry and subsequent glucose dips.

---

## 2. Dataset & Cohort Limitations

### A. Small Cohort Size ($N = 25$)
The HUPA-UCM dataset contains 25 participants. While the aggregate observation count is large (309,392 rows), the statistical unit for cross-participant generalization is 25 individuals. Findings cannot be generalized to the broader global population with Type 1 Diabetes without external multi-center validation.

### B. Severe Observation Duration Heterogeneity
- 22 participants contribute between 8 and 15 days of observation (~2,300 to ~4,100 records each).
- 3 longitudinal participants (`HUPA0026P`, `HUPA0027P`, `HUPA0028P`) contribute 90 to 574 days, representing over 75% of all pooled rows.
- *Evaluation Implication*: Pooled metrics can be skewed by the three long-duration participants. Group-level macro-averages provide a necessary balance by weighting each individual patient equally.

### C. Clinical Phenotypic Asymmetry
Participant `HUPA0009P` exhibited persistent hyperglycemia (mean glucose 193.7 mg/dL) and experienced **0 hypoglycemia episodes** throughout their 13-day recording period. In contrast, `HUPA0002P` spent 23.9% of their recording period in hypoglycemia. Models evaluated on single participants with extreme phenotypes must be interpreted with clinical awareness.

---

## 3. Sensor & Physiological Constraints

### A. Interstitial Physiological Lag
The Abbott FreeStyle Libre 2 measures glucose in the interstitial fluid rather than venous blood. Physiological diffusion introduces a recognized lag of 5 to 10 minutes between blood glucose and interstitial sensor readings. Rapid drops in blood glucose may not appear on the sensor until minutes later.

### B. Wearable Contextual Discontinuity
- Discrete meal boluses and carbohydrate logs are logged via the Medtronic pump interface at patient discretion. Unrecorded snacks or unannounced physical activity cannot be detected by retrospective features.
- Sleep stage and sleep efficiency telemetry from the raw Fitbit exports were excluded from real-time 5-minute features due to irregular participant coverage.

---

## 4. Statistical & Machine Learning Constraints

### A. Sliding Window Autocorrelation
Observations sampled every 5 minutes share 25 minutes of overlapping forward horizon with the preceding observation. While GroupKFold cross-validation prevents train/test leakage across participants, consecutive out-of-fold predictions within a participant are serially correlated.

### B. Personalisation Threshold Dynamics
Adapting population model probabilities via personal calibration shifts the output distribution from balanced logits (~0.50) down to the patient's individual baseline event frequency (~0.03). Using an unadjusted population threshold on recalibrated probabilities creates threshold mismatch unless operational decision thresholds are calibrated alongside the probabilities.
